WELCOME TO THE BURNER ACCOUNT

Welcome to The Burner Account, Our team's goal is to relay the anonymous confessions of APEC students, sharing it across APEC campuses, purely for enjoyment. We believe that, be it trivial or conflicting confessions need to be let out once in a while, so we decided to create a podcast where we read these confessions/messages, while keeping their identity hidden.

OUR PODCAST

In our stream we will perform a commentary, reading and forming positive opinions/advice about each confession as a collective with our viewers. Our aim is to help students let out untold secrets or thoughts kept to themselves, especially those in dilemma seeking guidance. We have ultimately decided to settle on this topic to build communication. Though we are here to vocalize these confessions, we do not in any form associate, validate nor agree with the actions/viewpoint with them.
